<h1>This is a phone holder for your camera</h1>
You can mount this phone holder on top of your camera, into the hot shoe mounting point.
It can be useful for shooting POV content, using your phone as a monitor, or enabling AI tracking for certain gimbals.

<h2>Versions:</h2>
<h3>v1:</h3>
The first verson can not tilt, and can only hold a phone up to 67mm wide.
