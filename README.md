<h1>This is a phone holder for your camera</h1>
You can mount this phone holder on top of your camera, into the hot shoe mounting point.
It can be useful for shooting POV content, using your phone as a monitor, or enabling AI tracking for certain gimbals.

<h2>Versions:</h2>
<h3>v1:</h3>
The 1st verson can not tilt, and can only hold a phone up to 67mm wide.
<h3>v2:</h3>
The 2nd verson can tilt, and can hold lot wider phones (up to 95mm).

<h2>Assembly instructions:</h2>
 <ul>
   
  <li>Print all the STL files.</li>
  <li>Screw the ring onto the screw part.</li>
  <li>Glue together the tilting part and the screw part.</li>
  <li>Slide the upper phone part into the tilting part.</li>
  <li>Screw the ring onto the upper phone part.</li>
   <li>It should look like the one in the <code>reference_image.png</code> </li>
</ul>
